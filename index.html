Gizmo→Anki

Turn your Gizmo folders into Anki decks

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Gizmo has no export and its shared links can't be read from another site. Open the link, copy the cards, paste them below.

One card per line (tab, ::, | or ;). A line starting with # starts a new deck (use :: for sub-decks).
